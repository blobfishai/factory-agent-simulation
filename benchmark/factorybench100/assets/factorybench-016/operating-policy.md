# Reroute assembly after a spindle failure — operating policy

Decision scope: assembly operation exposed to spindle failure.

Control rule: alternate must be active, qualified, and non-displacing. Establish the immutable source record and effective revision, then reconcile machine-hours needed to preserve the order, qualified alternate-cell hours with tooling and operator, and down primary-cell hours and protected alternate load from independent records. Do not treat a header total, filename, similar name, or unapproved alternative as evidence. The final mutation must be atomic and limited to the supported record and measure.

Required closeout records: record the selected option, committed completion, and binding constraint in the existing Control outcome cell; and post the decided option, date, constraint, alternatives, and Oracle reference in the existing operations thread.
