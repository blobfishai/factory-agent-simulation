# Correct payment terms from the signed contract — operating policy

Decision scope: payment terms on INV-0044.

Control rule: executed agreement, effective date, supplier site, and invoice must match. Establish the immutable source record and effective revision, then reconcile invoice amount governed by the contract, amount at the signed supplier-site terms, and value under an obsolete master term from independent records. Do not treat a header total, filename, similar name, or unapproved alternative as evidence. The final mutation must be atomic and limited to the supported record and measure.

Required closeout records: post the decided option, date, constraint, alternatives, and Oracle reference in the existing operations thread; and prepare the reply in the existing email thread and leave it as a draft for review; do not send it.
