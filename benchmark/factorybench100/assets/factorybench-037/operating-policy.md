# Reject water-damaged enclosures at inspection — operating policy

Decision scope: water-damaged enclosure receipt.

Control rule: accepted plus rejected must equal inspected and preserve carrier evidence. Establish the immutable source record and effective revision, then reconcile units presented for inspection, dry units passing the inspection plan, and water-damaged units documented by dock photos from independent records. Do not treat a header total, filename, similar name, or unapproved alternative as evidence. The final mutation must be atomic and limited to the supported record and measure.

Required closeout records: post the decided option, date, constraint, alternatives, and Oracle reference in the existing operations thread; and prepare the reply in the existing email thread and leave it as a draft for review; do not send it.
