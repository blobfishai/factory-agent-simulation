# Release the flight-test controller build — operating policy

Decision scope: flight-test controller work order.

Control rule: drawing, deviation, material, and dispatch release gates. Establish the immutable source record and effective revision, then reconcile released build quantity at the flight revision, components with released certs and nettable stock, and parts on quality hold or at the wrong revision from independent records. Do not treat a header total, filename, similar name, or unapproved alternative as evidence. The final mutation must be atomic and limited to the supported record and measure.

Required closeout records: prepare the reply in the existing email thread and leave it as a draft for review; do not send it; and post the decided option, date, constraint, alternatives, and Oracle reference in the existing operations thread.
