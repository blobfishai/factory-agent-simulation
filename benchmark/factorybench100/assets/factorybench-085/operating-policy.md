# Receive an advance-replacement return — operating policy

Decision scope: advance-replacement return.

Control rule: sales order, RMA, original serial, replacement serial, and receipt organization. Establish the immutable source record and effective revision, then reconcile serials expected under the return obligation, serial received with matching RMA and ownership, and replacement serial, accessory, or unrelated return from independent records. Do not treat a header total, filename, similar name, or unapproved alternative as evidence. The final mutation must be atomic and limited to the supported record and measure.

Required closeout records: record the selected option, committed completion, and binding constraint in the existing Control outcome cell; and post the decided option, date, constraint, alternatives, and Oracle reference in the existing operations thread.
