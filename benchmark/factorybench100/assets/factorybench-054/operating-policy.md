# Quarantine an expired chemical lot — operating policy

Decision scope: expired chemical lot.

Control rule: expiry, lot, location, and open reservations define containment scope. Establish the immutable source record and effective revision, then reconcile on-hand quantity in the named lot, quantity physically present and traceable to the expired lot, and other lots and quantity already consumed from independent records. Do not treat a header total, filename, similar name, or unapproved alternative as evidence. The final mutation must be atomic and limited to the supported record and measure.

Required closeout records: post the decided option, date, constraint, alternatives, and Oracle reference in the existing operations thread; and record the selected option, committed completion, and binding constraint in the existing Control outcome cell.
