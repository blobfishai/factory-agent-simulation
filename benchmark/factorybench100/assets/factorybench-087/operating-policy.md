# Attach a certificate of conformance to repair work — operating policy

Decision scope: certificate of conformance.

Control rule: immutable certificate identity and applicability to the repair material. Establish the immutable source record and effective revision, then reconcile candidate certificates, certificate matching supplier, lot, item, asset repair, revision, and signature, and expired, unsigned, wrong-lot, or similar-item certificates from independent records. Do not treat a header total, filename, similar name, or unapproved alternative as evidence. The final mutation must be atomic and limited to the supported record and measure.

Required closeout records: prepare the reply in the existing email thread and leave it as a draft for review; do not send it; and record the selected option, committed completion, and binding constraint in the existing Control outcome cell.
