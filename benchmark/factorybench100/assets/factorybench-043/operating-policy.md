# Release a hold after the supplier credit arrives — operating policy

Decision scope: existing invoice hold on INV-0043.

Control rule: credit, invoice, hold, and manager release approval must match. Establish the immutable source record and effective revision, then reconcile held variance amount, credit memo applied to the same invoice and reason, and unapplied or unrelated credit value from independent records. Do not treat a header total, filename, similar name, or unapproved alternative as evidence. The final mutation must be atomic and limited to the supported record and measure.

Required closeout records: send the scoped completion reply in the existing email thread; and record the selected option, committed completion, and binding constraint in the existing Control outcome cell.
