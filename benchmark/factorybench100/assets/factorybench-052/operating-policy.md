# Record failed dielectric-test samples — operating policy

Decision scope: dielectric-test inspection.

Control rule: sample results must drive lot disposition under the effective plan. Establish the immutable source record and effective revision, then reconcile samples tested under the plan, samples meeting voltage and leakage limits, and failed samples outside either limit from independent records. Do not treat a header total, filename, similar name, or unapproved alternative as evidence. The final mutation must be atomic and limited to the supported record and measure.

Required closeout records: append one dated decision row to the existing audit tab; do not overwrite prior entries; and add the resulting Oracle reference, option, date, and constraint as a comment on the existing case file.
