# Create incoming inspection for plated busbars — operating policy

Decision scope: plated busbar receipt.

Control rule: effective plan, lot, supplier, characteristic, and sample-size table. Establish the immutable source record and effective revision, then reconcile received busbars under the inspection plan, sample quantity required by lot size and severity, and units outside the lot or already inspected from independent records. Do not treat a header total, filename, similar name, or unapproved alternative as evidence. The final mutation must be atomic and limited to the supported record and measure.

Required closeout records: prepare the reply in the existing email thread and leave it as a draft for review; do not send it; and post the decided option, date, constraint, alternatives, and Oracle reference in the existing operations thread.
