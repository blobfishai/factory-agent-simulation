# Move unfinished production beyond period end — operating policy

Decision scope: unfinished production at cutoff.

Control rule: physical status, operation transactions, period cutoff, and revised dates. Establish the immutable source record and effective revision, then reconcile remaining operation-hours and quantity, work physically incomplete and unreported, and completed quantity and current-period actuals from independent records. Do not treat a header total, filename, similar name, or unapproved alternative as evidence. The final mutation must be atomic and limited to the supported record and measure.

Required closeout records: prepare the reply in the existing email thread and leave it as a draft for review; do not send it; and record the selected option, committed completion, and binding constraint in the existing Control outcome cell.
