# Replace an obsolete configuration before fulfillment — operating policy

Decision scope: obsolete controller configuration on SO-47004.

Control rule: engineering change, customer configuration, and serial breakpoint must all match. Establish the immutable source record and effective revision, then reconcile open order lines at the obsolete revision, lines inside the approved substitute effectivity, and completed or out-of-effectivity serials from independent records. Do not treat a header total, filename, similar name, or unapproved alternative as evidence. The final mutation must be atomic and limited to the supported record and measure.

Required closeout records: prepare the reply in the existing email thread and leave it as a draft for review; do not send it; and add the resulting Oracle reference, option, date, and constraint as a comment on the existing case file.
