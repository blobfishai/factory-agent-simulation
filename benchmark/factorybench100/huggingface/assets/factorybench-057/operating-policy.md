# Post a blind cycle-count adjustment — operating policy

Decision scope: blind cycle count.

Control rule: the book-to-count variance must pass the recount threshold, lot, reason-code, and supervisor controls. Establish the immutable source record and effective revision, then reconcile book quantity before count, independently counted physical quantity, and counted units with the wrong lot or unverified ownership from independent records. Do not treat a header total, filename, similar name, or unapproved alternative as evidence. The final mutation must be atomic and limited to the supported record and measure.

Required closeout records: add the resulting Oracle reference, option, date, and constraint as a comment on the existing case file; and post the decided option, date, constraint, alternatives, and Oracle reference in the existing operations thread.
