# Create a contamination-control program — operating policy

Decision scope: contamination-control maintenance program.

Control rule: signed standard, asset criticality, and quality approval define the program. Establish the immutable source record and effective revision, then reconcile assets named by the sanitation standard, active food-contact assets with approved frequencies, and inactive, non-contact, or already covered assets from independent records. Do not treat a header total, filename, similar name, or unapproved alternative as evidence. The final mutation must be atomic and limited to the supported record and measure.

Required closeout records: record the selected option, committed completion, and binding constraint in the existing Control outcome cell; and send the scoped completion reply in the existing email thread.
