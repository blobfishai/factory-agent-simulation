# Add new test-fixture capacity to an active order — operating policy

Decision scope: test-fixture resource requirement.

Control rule: fixture qualification, calibration, rate, and ECO approval. Establish the immutable source record and effective revision, then reconcile fixture-hours required by the ECO, qualified fixture-hours before test start, and uncalibrated or conflicting fixture-hours from independent records. Do not treat a header total, filename, similar name, or unapproved alternative as evidence. The final mutation must be atomic and limited to the supported record and measure.

Required closeout records: prepare the reply in the existing email thread and leave it as a draft for review; do not send it; and post the decided option, date, constraint, alternatives, and Oracle reference in the existing operations thread.
