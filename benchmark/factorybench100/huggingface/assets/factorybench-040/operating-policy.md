# Return mislabeled relays to the supplier — operating policy

Decision scope: mislabeled relay receipt.

Control rule: label nonconformance, lot, PO line, and RMA quantity must match. Establish the immutable source record and effective revision, then reconcile received quantity under the affected label, units covered by the supplier return authorization, and correctly labeled or already consumed units from independent records. Do not treat a header total, filename, similar name, or unapproved alternative as evidence. The final mutation must be atomic and limited to the supported record and measure.

Required closeout records: record the selected option, committed completion, and binding constraint in the existing Control outcome cell; and post the decided option, date, constraint, alternatives, and Oracle reference in the existing operations thread.
