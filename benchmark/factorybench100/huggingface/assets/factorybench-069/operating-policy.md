# Attach the released service bulletin to repair work — operating policy

Decision scope: released service bulletin.

Control rule: bulletin number, revision, release, model, serial, and work order must match. Establish the immutable source record and effective revision, then reconcile candidate bulletin revisions, released revision applicable to asset model and serial, and draft, superseded, or wrong-model bulletins from independent records. Do not treat a header total, filename, similar name, or unapproved alternative as evidence. The final mutation must be atomic and limited to the supported record and measure.

Required closeout records: append one dated decision row to the existing audit tab; do not overwrite prior entries; and add the resulting Oracle reference, option, date, and constraint as a comment on the existing case file.
